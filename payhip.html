Sound familiar?

You're not lazy.
Your brain just works differently.

Most productivity systems were built for brains that aren't yours. They assume you can just decide to start, that reminders work, that shame is motivating. None of that holds for ADHD.

You open the task, close it, and open it again fifteen times without starting.
You forget things you genuinely care about, not because they don't matter, but because your memory doesn't work the way you expect it to.
You know exactly what you should do. The wall between knowing and doing feels completely solid.
You burned out trying to keep up with a world that wasn't designed for your brain. And you've been told it's a you problem.

What's inside

Named, explained, and
made workable.

The ADHD Playbook names the things you've been experiencing without words for them. Then it gives you something to do about each one.

The Wall of Awful
The emotional weight that builds up around a task and makes starting feel impossible, even when the task itself is small. Understanding this changes everything about how you talk to yourself.
The Dopamine Menu
A personal toolkit of sounds, movement, competition, and novelty that actually helps your brain cooperate. Not a generic list, yours, built for how you work.
The External Brain
A Brain Dump, Command Centre, Morning Launch, and Evening Shutdown system that removes the burden of relying on memory for things memory was never meant to hold.
Time Blindness
ADHD brains don't experience time the way most systems assume. This chapter explains the science and gives you practical ways to work with it instead of against it.
RSD & Emotional Weather
Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria is real, intense, and often unrecognised. The Emotional First Aid Kit gives you a protocol for hard moments, built when your brain is calm enough to think.
The ADHD Operating Cycle
Eight phases that explain where you are right now, from activation and hyperfocus through burnout, collapse, and rebuild. Knowing your phase tells you what you actually need.
Burnout & the Failure Audit
When the system breaks down, this isn't a character flaw. The Failure Audit helps you rebuild from the smallest possible piece, without the shame spiral that usually follows.
Money, Relationships & Identity
Chapters on the Sunday Look (money without shame), Repair Conversations, medication, hormones, and who you are when the chaos settles. ADHD is a whole-life condition. This book treats it that way.

About the author

Written from the inside.

DI
Daniel Inimfon
Teacher · Adult with ADHD · Author

Daniel is a teacher who lives with ADHD. He is not a clinician, and this book makes no claim to be a medical resource. What it is: a practical guide built from personal experience, grounded in research, and written for the people who needed someone to say "I know exactly what that feels like" before offering a single tool. He wrote the book he wished existed when he was first trying to make sense of his own brain.
